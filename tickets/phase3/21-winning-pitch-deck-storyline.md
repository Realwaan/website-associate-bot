# Winning Pitch Deck Storyline and Live Stage Demo Rehearsal

**[PRIORITY]**

## Problem
Building impressive engineering is only half the battle; hackathons are won or lost in the 3-minute stage pitch. If the team explains complex tree theory without demonstrating a sharp business problem and live visual breakthrough, judges will score the project as a standard academic exercise rather than a breakthrough product.

## Potentially Related Files
- `README.md` - architecture diagrams, value proposition, and quickstart
- `src/App.tsx` / `app/static/index.html` - live stage presentation view
- `tests/test_api_endpoints.py` - verification of sample demo flows

## What to Fix
1. Structure the 3-minute pitch into 4 high-impact beats: The Enterprise Failure (Context Severance & Contract Hallucinations), The Innovation (3-Tier AST, Halo Cropping, and LanceDB Hybrid RRF), The Live Demo (30s visual breakthrough with Contradiction Hunter), and Business Impact ($1.2M audit liability savings).
2. Prepare a 5-slide visual deck: Title & Hook, The Architectural Flaw, AST-RAG Solution, Benchmark Matrix (sub-5ms vs 45s cloud RAG), and Market Roadmap.
3. Rehearse the live stage demo: 1-click sample load -> dynamic AST path lighting -> Table 4 visual crop -> Contradiction Alert card -> Defense Refusal comparison.
4. Showcase assistant-ui structured outputs (Score Breakdown, Data Table, Spec Sheet, Comparison Radar).
5. Ensure a dedicated teammate operates the stopwatch to guarantee finishing under the 3-minute buzzer.

## Acceptance Criteria
- 3-minute pitch script is finalized and rehearsed by all team members.
- Live demo executes in under 60 seconds without manual typing.
- 5-slide pitch presentation is formatted and exported to PDF as a backup.
- Q&A cheat sheet addresses technical architecture and commercial viability questions.
